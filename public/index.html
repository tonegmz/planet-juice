FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does coaching cost?

Group classes are $25 and 1-on-1 sessions start at $150. Monthly packages are $149 (Starter), $297 (Elite) and $497 (Champion). Online coaching starts at $199 a month.

Can I train with you online?

Yes. Online Coaching gives you an app-based program, regular check-ins and video feedback on your form. 1-on-1 sessions can also be done virtually.

Do I need my own gym equipment?

Let us know what you have access to on your free call, and your program will be built around it, whether you train in a gym, at home or online.

Is the 7-day meal plan really free?

Yes. Enter your first name and email in the meal plan form and we'll send it to your inbox.

Can I swap the milk in my smoothie?

Yes. Almond or oat milk is $0.50 extra on any smoothie. Protein smoothies can also be made with coconut milk for the same $0.50.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash. Delivery areas, prices and hours on DoorDash may differ from the shop.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 9am to 10pm, Saturday 9am to 9pm and Sunday 10am to 7pm. Holiday hours may vary.
